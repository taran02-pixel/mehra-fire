MEHRA FIRE

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

This policy explains how the Mehra Fire Android game handles information. The game does not require an account and does not contain ads, analytics, or in-app purchases.

Information stored on your device

Your fighter name, selected skin, and experience points are saved in the app's local WebView storage so your profile and progress remain available between sessions. This information is not sent to a Mehra Fire server. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes it.

Online play with a friend

Online play is optional. To connect, players exchange invite and answer codes themselves. The game uses WebRTC peer-to-peer networking and Google's public STUN service at stun.l.google.com:19302 to establish the connection. The STUN service and the friend you connect to may receive your IP address and network connection details. The connected player can see your chosen fighter name and live game state. Mehra Fire does not run a matchmaking server or retain invite codes or game sessions.

Sharing and retention

Mehra Fire does not upload or retain your local profile data. Connection details are exchanged with the STUN service and the peer only as needed to establish and use an online session. Google's handling of information is governed by Google's own privacy terms.

Children

The game does not ask for a player's age or collect a child's name or contact details. Online play requires players to exchange connection codes outside the game; parents and guardians should supervise that activity.

Your choices

You can play without using online mode. You can remove locally stored profile information by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.
